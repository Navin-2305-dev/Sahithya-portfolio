About Sahithya

A lawyer who prepares for the hardest question first.

Sahithya is a well-regarded advocate known for careful research, persuasive argument and straight answers. She explains where a matter stands, what the realistic options are, and what each one will cost in time and effort.

Clients value her for clarity under pressure. Whether in court, at the negotiating table or in a contract review, she keeps the focus on the outcome the client actually needs.
